# Archive

> Short version: finished working folders, kept as a record of how the work went. Nothing here is current, and nothing here is edited.

## What belongs here

- Whole folders moved from [the working folder](../working/) when their work ended, under the same name.

## What does not belong here

- Decision records. They stay in [the decisions folder](../decisions/), even when a later record replaces them.
- Anything that is still true. That belongs in [a feature](../features/) or [a standard](../standards/).

## Archiving a folder

Move it with git, so its history follows it:

```sh
git mv docs/working/faster-search docs/archive/faster-search
```
