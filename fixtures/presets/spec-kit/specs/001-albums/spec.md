# Feature Specification: Photo albums

**Feature Branch**: `001-albums`  
**Created**: 2026-01-05  
**Status**: Implemented  
**Input**: User description: "group photos into albums"

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Make an album

A person picks photos and puts them in a new album.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The app MUST let a person make an album.
- **FR-002**: An album MUST hold at least one photo.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

- **SC-001**: A person makes an album in under a minute.
